# The Catalyst — Lore & Background

---

## The Organisation — The Corvanites / The Forum

A relatively new organisation of extremist terrorists carefully selected from society, dedicated to rewriting the world. They present publicly as **The Forum** — a philosophical society and workers' union. Only the inner circle knows them as the **Corvanites**. They operate outside any nation's jurisdiction, use materials and intel from an unconfirmed god of reality named Corvan, and wield Steampunk mechanisms charged by Lithean material. This gives them temporal powers — briefly manipulating time to their whims — and allows them to move effectively across Vel'Taspere.

Their inspiration dates to the **Xuronian Calamity** — an obscure series of events coinciding with the Sovereign-Gard War within the late enclosed years of the Monolith, involving Paladin Uther Vadaam, Lucidean Hunters dignitary Melkath Theomach, and Grand Seeker Rivellon Mercer of the Exactitude. The Corvanites treat the findings of this period as sole truth, ignoring how incomplete those findings may be.

**Two stated goals:**
- Rewrite time through acts of terrorism, shifting elements of the world to better fit strands of possibility gleaned by Codron Vorhaus
- Search for and collect Diodes to protect Corvan's influence and ultimately enable the rewrite

> *"Not many nations allow terrorists to be their leaders. Yet so many allow their leaders to be terrorists."*
> — Attributed to the Executor

---

## Corvan

An unconfirmed god of reality. Only the Corvanite upper echelon knows of his existence. He manipulates Vorhaus and the Corvanites into harbouring the reality he wishes — one free from Xuros. His presence is felt throughout the Catalyst but never stated aloud.

---

## Codron Vorhaus — The Executor

The hidden figure at the top of the Corvanites. After watching his kingdom of Urathor shatter and the people in his life vanish, Codron concluded the world cannot be saved — it is fracturing from within and must be repaired at its core. He believes himself to be the **fourth coming of the Trace Evident**. His connection to Corvan allowed him to scale his operation to true devastation.

He resides in a cloaked tower off the coast of Commaloire in Dakare Bay, managing Forum forces from a Lithean control centre at the tower's peak. From here he reads different strands of possibility for Vel'Taspere, sending Corvanites out to commit acts of devastation in the hope of breaking down barriers in the world and rewriting time to fit his vision. He does not care for the atrocities committed, seeing them as a necessary evil.

To disguise his identity while remaining active in politics, he uses the same method as the late **Exarch Mordric Commel** — continuously presenting as the newest generation of the Vorhaus family, an influential power within Commaloire acting as the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. This keeps him close to real-world political strings while remaining hidden. It becomes increasingly obvious across the campaign that Commaloire's Minister of Foreign Affairs is always nearby and moves unusually fast.

Ultimately, Vorhaus wishes to rewrite time to save long-lost loved ones. He manipulates the greater Corvanites as a terrorist organisation in service of this personal grief.

---

## The Catalyst — The Tower

A black monolithic tower on a hidden island in Dakare Bay, off the coast of Commaloire. The island does not appear on any map. The tower is cloaked by Lithean crystal array and hums with Corvan's influence. Lithean crystal veins run from the tower's base across the volcanic black rock of the island like roots.

Eight floors, each with a distinct identity — from the civic veneer of the Forum's Vestibule at the base to the Lithean CONTROL at the peak where Codron works the strands of possibility. The tower functions as a machine, a monument, and a physical expression of Codron's mind and ideology.

---

## The Diodes

Artefacts gathered throughout the campaign. The Corvanites collect them to protect Corvan's influence and enable the time rewrite — a certain number are required. Players can destroy them, keep them, or have them taken. Those collected by the Corvanites are stored and studied in the Reliquary on Floor 4. The full array is assembled on the peak's dais for the ritual.

---

## The Trace Evident

A historical figure Codron believes himself to be the fourth coming of. Their findings within the Monolith form the ideological and scholarly backbone of the Corvanite belief system. The Hall of the Trace Evident on Floor 4 is dedicated to this history as Codron has curated and interpreted it.

---

## Vel'Taspere

The campaign world. The Corvanites believe it is entering a point of no return as gods desecrate it and humanity expands across continents. The Catalyst's peak projects a living probability map of Vel'Taspere — all its futures and failures visible at once through the Strand Dome.
